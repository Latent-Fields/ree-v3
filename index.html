REE V3 / active implementation

Test the architecture where it can fail.

REE V3 is the current experiment substrate for the Reflective-Ethical Engine. It turns architectural claims into bounded, inspectable tests rather than presenting them as settled outcomes.

REE is exploratory research. No material here has been accepted for peer-reviewed publication. Results and prototypes are not validated safety systems, clinical tools, or evidence of general scientific truth.

Architecture at a glance

Three coupled systems, tested in public.

V3 keeps the architecture legible by separating the functions that maintain context, predict action-conditioned consequences, and constrain commitment.

Persistent context

E2
